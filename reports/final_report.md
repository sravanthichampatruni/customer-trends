
Q1. What is the total revenue generated by male vs. female customers?

OBSERVATION & STRATEGIC INSIGHT (Q1):
- Male customers represent the primary revenue driver for this business, generating $157,890 
  compared to $75,191 from Female customers.
- This indicates a significant demographic split, with male consumers accounting for 
  more than double the revenue generated by the female segment.
- Strategy: Allocate marketing resources and ad spend toward male-focused campaigns 
  and high-performing product lines to maximize returns. Concurrently, conduct consumer 
  research to identify and improve engagement within the female customer segment.

Q2. Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?

OBSERVATION & STRATEGIC INSIGHT (Q2):
- Out of 3,900 total transactions, 839 customers used a promotional discount but 
  still maintained a final purchase amount higher than the company's average order value.
- This indicates that promotional discounts do not necessarily reduce overall revenue; 
  for a key segment of our customer base, discounts encourage higher-value purchases.
- Strategy: Group these 839 customers into a specific marketing segment. Target them 
  with exclusive promotional offers on high-margin or premium items to leverage 
  their higher spending limits.

Q6. Which 5 products have the highest percentage of purchases with discounts applied?
